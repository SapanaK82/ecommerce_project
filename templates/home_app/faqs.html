For Buyers

Q1: How can I purchase artwork from the website?
A: Simply browse the gallery, select a piece you like, and click “Add to Cart.” You can then proceed to checkout using a secure payment gateway.

Q2: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept all major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and UPI (India-specific). More options may be available at checkout.

Q3: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we offer worldwide shipping. Shipping charges and delivery times vary by location and will be displayed at checkout.

Q4: What if the artwork arrives damaged?
A: We take great care in packaging. If your artwork arrives damaged, please contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos, and we’ll resolve the issue promptly.

Q5: Can I return a purchased artwork?
A: Returns are accepted within 14 days for physical artworks if returned in original condition. Custom or digital art may not be refundable.

For Artists

Q6: How can I become a featured artist?
A: Sign up as a user, then apply to become an artist via your dashboard. Once your profile is approved, you can start listing your artworks.

Q7: Is there a fee to sell artwork?
A: Creating an account is free. We take a small commission on each sale to cover platform and transaction fees.

Q8: How do I upload my artwork?
A: After your artist profile is approved, go to your dashboard → “Add Artwork” → Fill in the artwork details and upload images.

Q9: How do I receive payments?
A: Payments are processed securely and transferred to your registered bank account or PayPal within 5–7 business days after a successful sale.

Q10: Can I edit or remove my artwork listings?
A: Yes, you can manage all your listings through your artist dashboard.

General Questions

Q11: Can I commission a custom artwork?
A: Yes! Many artists accept commissions. You can contact them directly via their profile or submit a request through our "Commission Artwork" page.

Q12: Is my personal information safe?
A: Absolutely. We follow industry-standard security practices and never share your information with third parties.

Q13: Who owns the copyright to the artwork after purchase?
A: The artist retains the copyright unless otherwise stated. Buyers get physical or digital ownership, not reproduction rights.

Q14: How can I contact support?
A: You can reach our support team via the Contact Us page or by emailing support@craffittiart.com.
